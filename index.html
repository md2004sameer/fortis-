About Us

Training People. Strengthening Safety Culture.

FORTIS SAFETY CENTER is a professional safety training institute based in Kushinagar, Uttar Pradesh, focused on practical safety education and HSE development.

Our classroom-based programs are designed to build knowledge, hazard awareness, risk-control understanding and professional confidence — connecting what is learned in the training room with what happens on a real workplace.

  • Practical and workplace-oriented learning
  • Career guidance and interview preparation

What courses does FORTIS SAFETY CENTER offer?

We offer NEBOSH IGC, IOSH, OSHA, First Aid, Fire Safety, HSE & Industrial Safety, Scaffolding Safety, Advanced Diploma in Industrial Safety and Corporate Safety Training. See the Courses section for a short description of each program.

Where is FORTIS SAFETY CENTER located?

We are located at Hotel, Kasia – Gorakhpur Rd, near Buddha Marg, Kushinagar, Uttar Pradesh, India. You can find directions from the Contact section.
